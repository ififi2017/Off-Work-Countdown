# DoneAt brand policy

Effective for new permissions granted with this policy, 2026-10-04.
Maintainer/contact: ififi2017, <hello@doneat.app>.

DoneAt, DoneAt Plus and the Open Day logo identify the official DoneAt
product. Software licenses do not grant permission to present an independent
build or service as official, sponsored, approved or supported by DoneAt.
This policy does not assert that these names are registered in every country.

## Independent projects

You may fork the software, modify it, charge for it and distribute it,
including through application stores, subject to its software licenses and
applicable law. For independently branded distributions, choose a distinct
product name and icon and replace official marketing artwork. Do not use
DoneAt or confusingly similar branding as your own product identity, or copy
our developer identity, support address or official-store presentation in a
way that misleads users about who operates your product.

You may truthfully state “Based on DoneAt open-source software” and link to
the original repository. Make clear that your project is independent and is
not affiliated with or endorsed by DoneAt. Retain required copyright and
license notices: attribution is not the same as product branding.

## References, reviews and local builds

Truthful references, links, criticism, comparisons and reviews are welcome.
You may reproduce official screenshots and the logo to identify the official
product in such material, provided you do not imply sponsorship or use them
as the identity of your own product. This permission covers only rights the
maintainer controls, not third-party artwork or device frames.

You may retain the bundled branding when building privately, testing or
preparing contributions to this project. You may link to official downloads
and redistribute unmodified official signed packages with their notices
intact, without implying that you are the publisher or official support.
Other permissions for branding may be requested at <hello@doneat.app>.
