# Contributing to DoneAt

Contributions are welcome. Follow AGENTS.md and the relevant platform guide
for architecture, localization and validation; submit a focused pull request.

By submitting original software or documentation for inclusion, you offer
that contribution under MPL-2.0, unless an existing file's separate license
requires otherwise or a different arrangement is explicitly agreed in writing.
You retain your copyright. This is not copyright assignment or permission
for the maintainer to relicense your contribution as proprietary software.
Only submit work you have the right to license, and identify third-party
material and preserve its copyright/license notices and source provenance.

Use `SPDX-License-Identifier: MPL-2.0` in the appropriate comment syntax for
new covered source files. Do not overwrite third-party license headers.
Files without a comment format are covered by the scope in LICENSING.md;
keep that notice and LICENSE with copies of those files.

Artwork has different terms in ASSETS.md. A pull request adding artwork must
state its creator, source and proposed license explicitly; submitting an
image does not silently assign its copyright or grant exclusive brand rights.
Confirm its permission before merging. Public independent releases should
use distinct branding as explained in TRADEMARKS.md.
